# Week 4

## What I worked on
This week I worked more on Mini Project 1, which is a personal website for my sister. I used what I learned from interviewing my client to make the proposal and PRD more specific. I also started planning the layout of the website before continuing with the code.

## What gave me trouble, and where I am still stuck
I had some trouble turning everything from the interview into a clear website plan. My sister has experience in both interior design and finance, so I am still figuring out how to organize her information without making the website feel too crowded.

## Something new I picked up
I learned that it is better to have a clear plan before asking AI to start building. Writing the proposal and PRD first helped me think more about who the website is for and what information actually needs to be included.

## Something I noticed about working with AI this week
I noticed that AI gives better results when I give it specific information from the client instead of just asking it to make a website. I also had to change some of its suggestions because they sounded too general and did not really match what my sister wanted.
