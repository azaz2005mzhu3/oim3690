## One Thing I Did Not Expect

One thing that surprised me was hearing Mario describe himself as someone who is not a “tech person.” I had always assumed that working at Google, especially in AI, required a very strong technical background. He explained that there are different sides to the business: some people build the technology, while others focus on selling it and helping customers understand its value.

This helped ease some of my anxiety about pursuing a career in AI. As a business student, I sometimes worry that I do not have enough technical knowledge to enter the industry. His perspective helped me realize that understanding customers, communicating clearly, and connecting technology to business needs are also valuable skills. I still want to develop my technical skills, but I now feel more confident that my business background could give me a meaningful way to contribute to the AI industry.
