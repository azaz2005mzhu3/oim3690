# Week 5 Notes

## What I worked on

This week, I mainly focused on my Mini Project 1. I continued working on the personal website I am building for my sister, who is also my client for this project. After the showcase, I showed her the current version of the website and asked for her feedback. We talked about what she liked, what she thought could be improved, and what changes would make the website feel more like her.

I also looked at the feedback from class and compared my website with some of the other websites that my classmates created. Seeing everyone's projects gave me more ideas about design, layout, and how different websites can be built for completely different purposes.

## What gave me trouble, and where I am still stuck

One thing I am still working on is finding the right balance between what I personally think looks good and what my client actually wants. Since I am building the website for my sister, I realized that my own preferences should not always be the final decision. I need to listen to her feedback and think about the website from her perspective.

I am also still learning how to make changes without making the website feel too complicated. Sometimes AI can give me many ideas at once, but not every suggestion actually fits the purpose of the website.

## Something new I picked up

One thing I learned this week is how important feedback is in the website-building process. During the showcase, I got to see many different websites and hear people talk about their design choices. It was interesting because even though we are all in the same class and using similar tools, everyone's final website looked very different.

I also learned that looking at other people's websites is actually a good way to improve my own. I can notice small things that work well, such as how information is organized, how images are used, or how easy the website is to navigate, and then think about whether similar ideas would make sense for my own project.

## Something I noticed about working with AI this week

The biggest thing I noticed this week was how powerful AI actually is for web development. During the showcase, seeing everyone share their websites made me realize that AI can help us build something that looks like a real website surprisingly quickly. A lot of things that would normally take a long time to code can now be created or changed much faster with AI.

At the same time, this made me realize that knowing how to work with AI is becoming even more important. If AI can generate a website so easily, the important skill is not just knowing how to write every line of code by yourself. It is also knowing how to communicate what you want, understand what AI gives you, recognize when something is wrong, and make good decisions about what to keep or change. I think learning how to control and work with AI effectively is becoming just as important as learning the technical skills themselves.
